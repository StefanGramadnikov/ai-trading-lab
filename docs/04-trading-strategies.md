# 04 — Trading strategy hypotheses

**Status:** Market coverage and comparison requirement APPROVED. Individual strategies are **UNVALIDATED RESEARCH HYPOTHESES**.

## Common strategy interface

Each strategy must declare: `strategy_id`, semantic version, market type, required signals, minimum data quality, decision horizon, entry/exit logic, estimated costs, parameter ranges, data provenance and known failure modes. Output `TradeProposal` or `Abstain`. None may bypass deterministic risk/execution rules.

## Solana memecoin strategy candidates

| Strategy | Hypothesis (not a fact) | Minimum defensible inputs | Principal failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GRONK-inspired launch momentum | Rising participation/volume in young liquid tokens may precede continued appreciation | Pool age, time-series price/volume/tx counts, liquidity, on-chain concentration; holder-count data only if genuinely indexed | Rug pulls, lagging signals, severe spread/price impact |
| Breakout | High-quality price breakouts may continue | Historically sampled OHLC/quotes, volume, depth; stable breakout definition | False breakouts, feed lag |
| Trend following | Short-term momentum persists long enough to overcome costs | History, trend features, turnover/fee estimates | Reversals and churn |
| Mean reversion | Extreme deviations occasionally revert | Historical reference distribution, sufficient depth, exit assumptions | Continued collapse, toxic liquidity |

**GRONK guide (supplied by a third party):** suggests scanning DexScreener, rejecting concentrated/illiquid tokens, measuring six-hour volume against a 24-hour quarter average and closing if the ratio is below 0.20. The guide claims $89→$7,769.14 and gives personal anecdotes; **no verified trade ledger or reproducible dataset is supplied**. Its concrete numbers (5% largest-wallet threshold, 6% sizing ceiling, 0.20 volume exit, 8% prediction probability gap) are **candidate parameters for controlled testing, NOT approved live rules**. See [source review](15-gronk-source-review.md).

### Special memecoin cautions

- DexScreener pair creation timestamps may not equal token launch time. Discovery endpoints may omit launches.
- Solana `getTokenLargestAccounts` and `getTokenSupply` **do not provide unique holder counts**. Token accounts may belong to exchanges/pools; concentration calculation needs correct ownership semantics. Do not label an inferred or missing holder count as observed.
- Contracts can have freezing/mint authority, transfer rules and liquidity changes; risk screening cannot certify safety.
- A backtest using midpoint quotes may be substantially optimistic. Incorporate quote freshness, estimated route impact, latency, fees and failed exits.
- Token research prompts and websites may contain prompt injection; all evidence and tools are read-only.

## Prediction-market strategy candidates

| Strategy | Hypothesis | Required inputs | Failure modes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Probability mispricing | Independently calibrated probability meaningfully differs from net tradable implied odds | Exact event/outcome/settlement conditions; bid/ask depth, fees, resolution time; probability with provenance | AI overconfidence, ambiguous resolution, stale odds |
| Cross-venue discrepancy | Equivalent contracts occasionally quote materially different net prices | Demonstrated semantic equivalence, settlement definitions, fees, execution depth, settlement risk | Contracts differ, time mismatch, restricted access, cost exceeds gap |

The guide cites Polymarket Gamma and Kalshi market metadata and gives sample values, but does not establish that its `my_prob` estimates are calibrated. **A number generated by an LLM is not automatically an independent edge**. Evaluation must score eventual resolutions (e.g., Brier score, calibration, log loss) and compare forecast performance with a market-implied baseline. For long-dated unresolved contracts, report marked exposure separately from realized results.

## Parameter research policy

1. Establish fixed, documented candidate sets or a separate training process.
2. Backtest only on data that could have been known at decision time; capture delisted/failing tokens if feasible.
3. Use separate validation and untouched holdout periods. Avoid repeatedly optimizing against the final 30-day holdout.
4. Record spread, fees, turnover, liquidity participation, partial fills, missing data and failures.
5. Any strategy revision receives a new version and a new experiment identifier.
6. Favor fewer interpretable strategies over dozens of unfunded hypotheses.

## Initial implementation order (PROPOSED)

1. Collect both markets' read-only data and define valid market identity.
2. Build conservative, auditable paper execution before judging any strategy.
3. Implement rules-only GRONK-inspired launch-momentum **and** simple probability-mispricing research as reference baselines; add other families by measured opportunity/cost.
4. Add hybrid and multi-agent decision layers with matched-input ablation comparisons.

No strategy can be called profitable until its actual, held-out net results and uncertainty support that statement.
